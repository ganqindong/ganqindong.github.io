Causally Tested QoS-Floor Constraints for Decentralized SAGIN Access Association

Qindong Gan1, Zhenni Pan1, Tu Dac Ho2, Jun Wu1, and Shigeru Shimamoto1

1Waseda University   2Norwegian University of Science and Technology

IEEE CCNC 2027 · Accepted

Revised author manuscript (PDF, October 14, 2026)

Overview

This work develops a QoS pricing approach for decentralized vehicle access association in space–air–ground integrated networks. It incorporates per-vehicle expected-shortfall budgets to improve short-term service and uses matched control experiments to examine the role of price feedback, identifying when adaptation helps and the associated throughput and switching trade-offs.

Method

The approach incorporates per-vehicle expected-shortfall budgets into centralized training with decentralized execution. Training rewards and price updates use the same budget residual. Matched controls compare pricing with no pricing, intact with shuffled residual updates, and adaptive with frozen prices initialized at the same value.

Results

Across three training seeds with 12 vehicles, the shortfall-budgeted approach reduced outage by 66.3–86.0% relative to unconstrained MAPPO and increased the mean instantaneous minimum rate from 0.0116 to 0.2097 Mbps. Matched controls showed that service gains persisted under shuffled residual updates, while adaptive-versus-frozen comparisons identified budget-dependent benefits of price adaptation. The results demonstrate substantial short-term service improvements and clarify the associated throughput and switching trade-offs.
